## What it does

`egm-design` adapts to your starting point — a raw idea, an agreed architecture, or an existing design document — and maintains one canonical design with an explicit `Definition of Done` and separate delivery gates.

Unless you request drafting only, it dispatches risk-based fresh-context reviewers, resolves authorized local defects, runs targeted revalidation, and brings material trade-offs and final approval to you.

## When to use it

Use Design whenever you want to specify a feature, refactor, or architectural change before authorizing implementation separately.

## What you get

One maintained design document with contracts, decisions, verification criteria, and compact revision-bound review state — without duplicate briefs, drafts, or per-stage report files.

## Example

```text
/egm-design Design webhook delivery for this project.
```
